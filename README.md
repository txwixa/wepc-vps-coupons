## Choosing by the audience you're targeting

**Australian audience, TikTok Shop AU or local content.** AU Basic at $4.24 is the cheapest route onto an Australian IP. AU Standard at $12.56 runs on an EXETEL residential broadband IP, which is the better pick for going live rather than posting clips. On Chinese telecom networks, both may need transit.

**US accounts and live streaming.** US LA Basic at $5.23 gives a native US IP on 500 Mbps for account management and uploads. The 9929 Pro at $8.40 costs $3.17 more and buys CN2 GIA / 9929 routing, which is exactly what the 30% annual code attaches to. Streaming to a US audience is the case where that routing earns its price.

**Europe on a budget.** UK and DE entry plans both start at AUD $8.90, and both sell out. One listing describes the UK entry as a hosting IP rather than a residential one, worth checking if residential IPs are the reason you're shopping.

**Asia.** Japan is the cleanest option: native IPv4, three-network direct, no transit required. Korea's residential plans also run without transit. Singapore's residential plan handles posting fine, but livestreaming is where transit gets recommended, and Taiwan live streaming needs it outright. Thailand, Vietnam and Malaysia follow the same pattern for telecom users: the server works, the route doesn't, and the fix costs extra.

The US LA Advanced at $18.90 (4 cores, 2 GB RAM, 4 TB) is built for heavy multi-account operations. One account posting a few videos a day doesn't need it, and the extra $10.50 a month buys capacity you won't use.

## Before you pay: refund limits, stock and transit

**The refund window is 3 days and 10 GB of traffic.** That's enough to confirm the IP lands in the right country and that uploads go through. It is not enough for a multi-hour live stream test on a 2 TB plan, because a single session can eat most of the allowance. Past the window, refunds to your original payment method stop; what remains is a proportional credit to your account balance, which you can't withdraw.

**Stock moves.** The $8.90 UK and German tiers and several popular configurations regularly show zero inventory. The cart is the only place that reflects it.

**Transit is a separate line item.** Some regions perform fine on China Unicom or Mobile and poorly on telecom without an IEPL relay: Shenzhen to Hong Kong, 300 Mbps, 500 GB of traffic, up to three hops, billed at 2x in both directions, with SOCKS, HTTP and TLS disabled on it. Budget for it if your own connection or your audience is on telecom.

**No Shadowsocks on non-relay plans.** WePC states this outright, so pick a different protocol unless you're buying a relay.

## FAQ

**Can I use WePCMON90 and WePCYEAR80 together?** No. They apply to different billing cycles and the discounts don't stack. Choose the cycle first, then the matching code.

**Do the codes on the UK coupon sites work here?** No. Those belong to the other company using the WePC name. The four codes at the top of this article are the ones the VPS checkout recognises.

**Which code should I use for a one-month test?** WePCMON90 on any general plan, or WePC9929MON85 if you're testing the Los Angeles 9929 route. Keep the test inside 3 days and under 10 GB if you may want the money back.

**Can I refund an annual plan?** Same 3 days and 10 GB, and tighter in practice because the full year is already paid. Buy monthly first if you haven't used the provider before.

**Why was my code rejected?** Region and model exclusions. Taiwan, Singapore, parts of the US residential series and special routes have been reported as not accepting the general codes. If a code fails on the plan you want, try the other billing cycle's code before giving up on the discount.
